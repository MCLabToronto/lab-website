Our mission

The MCLab is a multidisciplinary and collaborative research team committed to advancing a rigorous, neuroaffirmative understanding of neurodiversity.

Multidisciplinary

We bring together complementary perspectives to better understand diverse developmental experiences and needs.

Collaborative

Our projects combine neuroimaging, cognitive neuroscience, participatory research, and large-scale data analysis.

Neuroaffirmative

We aim to produce rigorous knowledge with meaningful scientific and community impact.
