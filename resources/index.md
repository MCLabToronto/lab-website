## Posters and handouts

### Five ways to support autistic patients

Developed from our article in the *Canadian Medical Association Journal* (CMAJ), this poster summarizes five practical ways health care professionals can better support autistic patients. It is designed to be displayed in hospitals, clinics, and other health care settings.

Read the [CMAJ article](https://doi.org/10.1503/cmaj.250152) by Louisa Lok Yee Man, Adeline Lacroix, and Meng-Chuan Lai.
