description: >
  Carleigh is a PhD candidate studying flow, a state of deep focus and immersion experienced across many activities. Her research focuses on autistic and ADHD populations, looking at related states like hyperfocus, monotropism, and inertia, and what supports or disrupts them. She also examines how sensory features and task design shape attention, and whether environmental or task-based interventions can improve concentration and autonomy in school and work settings.
