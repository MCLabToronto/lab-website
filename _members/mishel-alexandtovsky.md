description: >
  Mishel is an autistic autism researcher at the University of Toronto and the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health. She is conducting participatory work that critiques cognitive tasks commonly used in autism research. She also collaborates on studies of neurodiversity affirming care, camouflaging, and cross-neurotype evaluation. The overarching goal of her research is to improve the understanding of autistic people and, through that, help foster greater acceptance, better supports, and autistic flourishing. Alongside her research, Mishel enjoys curating collections, travelling to new and familiar places, and listening to the same songs on repeat.
